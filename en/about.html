ONE PROJECT · TWO BRANCHES · ONE PASSION

PASSION BRINGS US TOGETHER. RACING MAKES US GROW.

ATX was born from a simple desire: to continue a collective adventure around virtual motorsport, driven by commitment, ambition and team spirit.

01 / THE TEAM

ATX Motorsport

After leaving the French team I raced for, I decided to create a project more closely aligned with my ambitions and vision. Together with two former members who shared that motivation, I founded ATX Motorsport to build a dynamic, sustainable organisation focused on the future.

Our goal is to bring passionate drivers together, help them progress and compete in a serious, welcoming and respectful environment. Recruitment and team development are central to that approach.

02 / THE ORGANISATION

ATX Racing

ATX Racing is the organisational branch of the project. It grew from our desire to create our own events, with formats that better reflect our vision of competition.

Standalone races, structured championships and accessible events: we want to create engaging competitions where the community can meet and race.

03 / Our vision

A RACE ALWAYS WITHIN REACH.

In the longer term, we plan to add servers offering regular races throughout the day — every hour or every two hours — around a 45-minute format.

Those races could form a permanent championship, with registration and tracking directly through this website. The project is still developing, but its ambition is clear: a regular, organised and rewarding racing experience.

ATX / MOTORSPORT + RACING

Two complementary branches

ATX Motorsport brings drivers together and carries the team colours in competition. ATX Racing creates and organises the events where the community meets and competes.
