ACC / DRIVER HUB

Rules

Sporting rules common to all ACC races. Event-specific details appear on its page.

01 / ACC

Driving standards

Race respectfully and predictably. Avoidable contact, dangerous line changes, deliberate braking and unsporting moves are prohibited.

02 / ACC

Start and first corner

Adjust braking for traffic and respect cars alongside. A deliberate collision may lead to immediate disqualification.

03 / ACC

Race control

After a multi-car crash in the first three corners, race control may order one restart. A VSC message then requires the pit limiter and a maximum speed of 50 km/h.

04 / ACC

WGT Sprint

Two drivers per crew and two 60-minute races. One stop with a driver change is required; stints are limited to 35 minutes. Tyres and fuel are optional.

05 / ACC

WGT Endurance

Crews of 1 to 6 drivers. Each stint is limited to 45 minutes; tyre and fuel strategy is up to the crew.

06 / ACC

Daily Race

A 60-minute race and one mandatory stop between minutes 25 and 35. Tyres and fuel are optional.

07 / ACC

Ballade ATX

A 90-minute single-make race with two mandatory stops, tyre changes and refuelling.

08 / ACC

After an incident

Rejoin only when safe and respect blue flags. Submit protests via the event’s SimGrid procedure.
